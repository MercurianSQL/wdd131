About Me & My Pug Suca

A group of Pugs is called a Grumble. That term fits our family of 4 humans and 1 pug very nicely. Suca is a standard pug. She is a standard color type, fawn. That means her coat is a light brown or tan color, but her ears and face have significantly darker marks. Her ears are non-standard. Breed standard is button ears. Her ears are flying. Suca has a standard tail type. It curls like a cinnamon roll. Most of Suca's claws are clear, not black. This theoretically makes them easier to keep trim. 🐾

Shang Dynasty Historical Banner

The Shang Dynasty was an ancient Chinese dynasty that lasted from approximately 1600 BC to 1046 BC. This is the time period when Pugs are believed to have been bred as companions to the emperor in ancient China. 🐾
